## 6. Research Notes and Reliable Sources

*   **Fermi Paradox Origin:** The informal lunchtime conversation took place in the summer of 1950 at Los Alamos National Laboratory, involving Enrico Fermi, Edward Teller, Herbert York, and Emil Konopinski.
*   **The Great Filter Concept:** Formulated by economist Robin Hanson in his 1996 essay *"The Great Filter – Are We Almost Past It?"*
*   **Drake Equation:** Created by Dr. Frank Drake in 1961 as a heuristic argument to estimate the number of active, communicative civilizations in the Milky Way galaxy.
*   *Fact-Checking Note:* Exact values for the number of Earth-like planets remain estimated and model-dependent based on data from NASA's Kepler and TESS missions.
